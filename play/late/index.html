Iritor — 2004 walkable scene

Explore the recovered hills alongside the two actors created by the surviving server prototype.

Click the scene, then use W/X or Up/Down to move, A/D or Left/Right to turn, and J/L to step sideways. S opens statistics, Q toggles the console, and Enter starts chat. The number pad moves the camera. Hold the buttons below for movement.

This scene runs recovered February 2004 animation, terrain and movement code. The original sky dome and two moving cloud layers are included. Look up shows them with an adapted camera preset. The recovered follow camera is used; browser graphics and input are adapted. The default scene objects and collision engine are included, along with the two original human-shaped NPCs and their preset movement. Their prototype AI has no combat behavior. The original console, information bar and two-tab statistics shell are included. The half-full bars and empty statistics fields are original prototype defaults. Chat stays in this local scene; combat, inventory and multiplayer are not included.
