Play the later prototype

A smaller download. Desktop keyboard recommended.

W/X moves, A/D turns and J/L strafes. S opens statistics, Q opens the console. Its empty statistics fields and unfinished combat reflect the surviving prototype.

Both versions run locally in your browser, without an account or game server. Rendering, input and local simulation have been adapted for the browser. Core saves retain your character and equipment in a fresh encounter, rather than saving the entire world. Use its Export button to keep a portable copy.

About this restoration

Iritor was developed at Wootsoft. These backups preserve development work from 2001–2004. This project brings selected builds back to life, documents their history and keeps their original artwork and behaviour where possible.

The Core source/art pairing is not dated precisely. The February 2004 snapshot marks the end of the preserved repository, rather than a confirmed date for the company's closure. The film distinguishes source dates, original footage and later compatibility work.

These browser editions are representative single-player restorations. Core's native item transactions, some character options, sound, scenery collision and further rendering effects remain incomplete. No new combat or statistics system has been invented for the unfinished later branch.
